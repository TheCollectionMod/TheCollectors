The Collectors is a mod that expands the catalog of vanilla items, aiming to improve the experience and importance of existing ores and enriches the world's lore through collectible paintings that uncover the hidden backstories of your town NPCs (currently featuring the Meteorman and the Ninja). 

It currently adds:
- Multiple NPCs (5 Town NPCs, 12 enemies with banners and 61 critters).
- +200 Items (Including Weapons, Armor pieces, Furniture, Blocks, Fruits, Paintings, Tools, Ores, Ammo, Wings, Vanity pieces, Walls, Pets, a Mount, an Accessory and more!!).
